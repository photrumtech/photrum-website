PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: June 2026

Photrum Technologies (“Photrum”, “we”, “our”, “us”) provides cybersecurity training, consulting, and digital learning services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website photrum.com and any related services.

What data we collect

We collect information that helps us deliver and improve our services. This includes:

  • Information you provide. Name, email, phone number, organisation details, messages you send us through forms, event registrations, or newsletter sign-ups.
  • Automatically collected data. IP address, browser details, device type, pages visited, time spent, and basic analytics data through cookies or similar tracking technologies.
  • Payment and billing details. Only when you purchase a course, consultation, or event. Payments are processed through secure third-party providers and we never store full card details.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide requested services.
  • To manage event registrations, consultations, and training programs.
  • To improve the website and understand how users interact with our content.
  • For security, fraud monitoring, and platform protection.
  • For marketing only when you voluntarily opt in.

How we store and protect your data

We use industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your information. This includes encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews. Only authorised team members access your data.

Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We only share your information with:

  • Trusted service providers like analytics tools or payment processors.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.
Your rights Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, update, or delete your information. You can contact us at any time to exercise these rights.
